Our colours today, and three decisions

We checked every colour the website, the admin panel and the three apps use. Here is what we found and the three decisions we need. Nothing changes until you decide.

The Review board opens with Where things stand: what was wrong before, what exists now, what it means when it ships, and what is waiting on you.

What we have today

The company colours live on the website. Each app has its own colours, set in the admin panel (Manage → Bundles → Colors for the app as a whole; Manage → Products → Colors for each product inside it). Changing a colour there changes the app the next time it opens; no update through the app stores is needed for most of the screen, and a small app update finishes the rest.

The three decisions

1 · Company colours

Recommended Keep navy and gold, exactly as before. Everything we built keeps them; nothing new is added.

Say yes, or tell us what you would rather have.

2 · One colour per app

Recommended Each app gets one colour, used everywhere for that app: in the app, on the website, on its tile and its icon.

Fire Exam Prep#D60000EMT / EMS#1F6B36Firefighter Interview Prep#33C3FF

These are colours the apps already use today; we are choosing, not inventing. Once chosen, we set them in the admin panel and tidy the few odd values, and the website, tiles and icons follow.

For EMT and EMS this is the same question the Review board asks in its technical form: keep #008000, the green the app shows today, or move to #1F6B36, the darker green the EMT and EMR pages on the website show. Keep and nothing customers hold changes; move and the app re-tints the next time it opens, with no app store update.

Say yes, or pick a different one of the swatches shown above for any app.

3 · The logo and the website look

Recommended Approve the redrawn logo (same navy and gold, drawn so it stays sharp from a browser tab to an app icon) and the newer website look that is already built and waiting. The logo comes in two versions: A on a plain tile, or D inside the shield outline of the previous logo. Both, and what the mark means, are on the review board.

Say A or D for the logo, and keep the newer website look or go back to the earlier look.

What happens once you decide

  • We set each app's colour in the admin panel (bundle and products), ship the small app update for the few screens that do not follow the admin yet, and check the apps on a phone simulator; new screenshots go on the website and, later, the app stores.
  • The website, product tiles and app icons are regenerated from the chosen colours; app icons reach customers only with the next app release.
  • The decision is written down once, in the project record, so it is not re-litigated.

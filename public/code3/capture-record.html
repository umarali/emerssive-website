How the app screens were captured

The record of the screenshot session, kept so anyone can see the screens came from the real apps running in Apple's simulator, driven by the agent, and not from a person's phone. Every image below is the untouched simulator capture (device clock and status bar included, dev-mode toasts silenced); the six that reached the website were cropped and resized from these. Full-size originals sit beside this page in raw/.

The Review board opens with Where things stand: what was wrong before, what exists now, what it means when it ships, and what is waiting on you.

01What ran

  • Fire Exam Prep, EMS Exam Prep and Firefighter Interview Prep were built from their own product branches of the mobile repo (React Native 0.76.9) with Xcode 26 and installed in the iPhone 17 Pro simulator, iOS 26.1. Build results:
fire: ** BUILD SUCCEEDED **
emt: ** BUILD SUCCEEDED **
fip: ** BUILD SUCCEEDED **
  • Signed-out screens were captured against the live API. For the signed-in screens (results, build a quiz, unlocked libraries) the apps were pointed at a local copy of the backend seeded from the live API's own public data (products, bundle themes, module titles, the audit questions and flashcards) with one test customer holding subscriptions; nothing was written to production. Seed summary:
[seed] users=1 products=19 bundles=3 contents=12 questions=100 flashcard_items=100 subs=3
LOGIN shots-customer@example.com (local server only)
  • Taps and typing were sent to the simulator programmatically (mouse events mapped into the simulator window; hardware-keyboard keystrokes); the quiz was driven by a script that reads each screenshot to find the answer rows. The quiz was capped to five questions locally for the driven attempts, and the file was restored before the fire-quiz and ems-quiz captures (they show 1 of 50).
